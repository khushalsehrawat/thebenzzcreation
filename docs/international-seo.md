# International SEO

One worldwide English site at https://www.thebenzzcreations.com. The operational location remains New Delhi, India. Primary enquiry markets are the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, UAE (including Dubai) and India; enquiries elsewhere remain welcome.

`/global-services` explains discovery, time-zone overlap, written reviews, market requirements, integration dependencies and launch responsibilities. Market examples are discovery considerations, not delivered functionality or regulatory advice. This page is linked from the homepage, footer, About, services and FAQs.

Country/city pages were deliberately not created: the repository does not contain sufficient original regional experience or case evidence. No offices, foreign teams, fixed delivery promises or regional prices were invented. Overseas pages should require original approved projects, market-specific operating information and a distinct user purpose before publication.

No hreflang is emitted because no equivalent localized variants exist. English content is marked lang=en. Country mentions are not language alternatives. If genuine translations are introduced, add self/reciprocal alternate links with valid codes and distinct canonical URLs; do not redirect visitors solely by IP.

References: [Google localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions), [Google international sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites/).

Use Search Console country/query reports and actual enquiry quality to validate intent; keyword hypotheses are not measured search demand.
