Provider selection articles link to the existing platform and cost guides rather than reproduce them. The ERP provider, cost and partner articles answer separate buying decisions and link to each other. The general software cost guide excludes the ERP migration depth covered by its separate guide.

- Website cost explains how to budget. The brief guide explains the inputs a supplier needs.
- The broad platform guide compares CMS/framework/commerce responsibilities. The commerce guide follows products, payments, stock, orders, shipping and returns.
- The startup cornerstone sequences systems. The existing buy-or-build guide examines a particular failing workflow.
- The AI guide treats interpretation, evaluation and approval. It does not present every deterministic integration as AI or imply guaranteed savings.
- The mobile article covers app-specific platform and operational costs rather than duplicating the website budget article.

## Metadata and dates

Every public guide has a unique title, description and H1, index/follow metadata, one absolute canonical, Open Graph and Twitter image metadata, one shared entity graph and appropriate breadcrumbs. New guides use 20 November 2026 for authored/reviewed dates; preserved guides retain 18 November 2026. Article schema and visible dates use the same data fields.

Each article has a 1200×630 social JPEG and 480/800/1200-pixel WebP artwork. Reading times are computed, not manually invented. No localized counterpart URLs exist, so no hreflang is emitted. Any future localized edition needs actual equivalent content and reciprocal relationships before adding hreflang.

## Missing external evidence

No connected Search Console, Bing Webmaster, analytics-property or paid keyword data was used. Do not describe these targets as proven demand or achieved visibility. Review actual queries and qualified enquiries after deployment before expanding the cluster.
